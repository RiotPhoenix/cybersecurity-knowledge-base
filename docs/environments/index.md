# Environments

The same tool can be appropriate for a hobbyist and inappropriate for a regulated enterprise —
or the reverse. Every tool entry therefore rates **personal suitability** and **enterprise
suitability** separately, and these tracks collect environment-specific guidance.

| Track | Typical constraints |
|---|---|
| [Personal and Home Lab](personal/index.md) | Limited budget and hardware, single administrator, learning focus, isolated lab networks. |
| [Enterprise](enterprise/index.md) | Scale, change control, compliance and audit, separation of duties, support contracts, high availability. |
